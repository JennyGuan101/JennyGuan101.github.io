**Leadership Researcher · Writer · Founder of LEO**

I am an undergraduate student at Purdue University, majoring in Business Analytics and Information Management with a minor in Business Economics, and a member of the John Martinson Honors College.

My research interests lie at the intersection of servant leadership, educational opportunity, mentorship, leadership development, and experiential learning.

I am particularly interested in how educational organizations, mentoring relationships, and experiential learning expand access to developmental opportunities for students and emerging leaders.

My writing has appeared in *Leader to Leader* and *The Journal of Values-Based Leadership*, among other publications.

I am also the founder of the Leadership Enhancement Organization (LEO), an international initiative connecting students, scholars, and experienced leaders through mentorship, dialogue, and collaborative learning.

In addition, I serve as a core organizer and co-author,  of the Servant Leadership 2.0 series, a three-volume collaborative publishing initiative bringing together more than 100 contributors, including scholars, university leaders, executives, and emerging leaders.
